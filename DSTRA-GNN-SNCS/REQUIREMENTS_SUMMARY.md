## Critical Requirements

### 1. Extended Version with 30% New Material
- **MUST**: Include at least 30% new material compared to the conference version
- **MUST**: Include an appendix that clearly explains:
  - Why the extended version includes at least 30% new material
  - Point out the relevant parts of the manuscript
  - This appendix is for review only and won't be included in the final published version

### 2. Document Format Changes

#### Current Format (IEEE):
- Uses `IEEEtran.cls` class
- IEEE conference format
- IEEE bibliography style

#### Required Format (SN Computer Science):
- **Option 1**: Word document (.docx format)
- **Option 2**: LaTeX using Springer Nature's LaTeX template (recommended)
- Must use Springer Nature's official template, NOT IEEE format

### 3. Abstract Requirements

#### Current Abstract:
- Single paragraph, unstructured
- Approximately 200 words

#### Required Abstract:
- **Structured abstract** (150-250 words)
- Must be divided into four sections:
  1. **Purpose** (stating the main purposes and research question)
  2. **Methods**
  3. **Results**
  4. **Conclusion**

### 4. Keywords

#### Current:
- 5 keywords provided

#### Required:
- 4-6 keywords (current count is acceptable)
- May need adjustment to align with journal scope

### 5. Title Page Requirements

#### Current Title Page:
- Basic author information
- Email addresses
- No ORCID IDs

#### Required Title Page:
- Author name(s)
- Affiliation(s) with full address information
- Clear indication of corresponding author with active email
- **16-digit ORCID ID** for each author (if available)
- Address information will be published

### 6. New Required Sections

#### Statements and Declarations Section
**MUST** include under heading "Statements and Declarations":
- **Competing Interests**: Authors must disclose financial or non-financial interests directly or indirectly related to the work
- Other declarations as applicable (see submission guidelines)

#### Author Contributions Statement
**MUST** include author contribution statement:
- Can use free text format OR CRediT taxonomy
- Examples provided in guidelines:
  - Free text: "All authors contributed to the study conception and design. Material preparation, data collection and analysis were performed by [name], [name] and [name]. The first draft of the manuscript was written by [name] and all authors commented on previous versions of the manuscript. All authors read and approved the final manuscript."
  - CRediT taxonomy: "Conceptualization: [name], …; Methodology: [name], …; Formal analysis and investigation: [name], …; Writing - original draft preparation: [name, …]; Writing - review and editing: [name], …; Funding acquisition: [name], …; Resources: [name], …; Supervision: [name], …."

#### Required:
- Must follow Springer Nature reference format

### 8. Figures and Tables

#### Required:
- Must follow Springer Nature figure/table guidelines

### 9. Peer Review Process

- **Single-blind peer review** (reviewers know authors, authors don't know reviewers)
- Minimum of 2 reviews for each article type

### 10. Content Alignment with Special Issue Theme

The special issue focuses on:
- **Pervasive digital services** for people's well-being, inclusion, and sustainable development
- Services that run on heterogeneous devices and permeate daily life
- Accessible and personalized experiences
- Contributing to well-being and preserving the Planet

**Consider**: May need to emphasize how DSTRA-GNN contributes to:
- Sustainable transportation (reducing congestion, emissions)
- Well-being (improved travel time, reduced stress)
- Inclusion (accessible navigation services)
- Pervasive digital services (mobile apps, navigation systems)

### 11. Additional Considerations

- **LLM/AI Usage**: If any AI tools were used, must be documented in Methods section
- **Data Availability**: May need to address data sharing policies
- **Ethical Standards**: Must comply with Springer Nature ethical standards
- **Permissions**: Need permissions for any previously published figures/tables/text
